Shailly's Tattoo Studio respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, why we use it, and the choices available to you.

01

Information we collect

When you contact us or request an appointment, we may collect:

  • Your full name, email address, and phone number.
  • Your preferred studio, appointment date, and appointment time.
  • Your tattoo idea, references, placement, size, and related requirements.
  • Age-verification information when required for our services.
  • Basic device, browser, referral, and website-use information from analytics tools.

02

How we use your information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to enquiries and arrange consultations or appointments.
  • Understand your tattoo requirements and prepare for your visit.
  • Send relevant appointment reminders and follow-up communication.
  • Provide customer support, improve our services, and meet legal obligations.
Our commitment

We do not sell or rent your personal information for third-party marketing.

03

Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the website and Google AdSense to display advertising. These services may use cookies or similar technologies to process information such as pages visited, time on site, device type, general location, and referral source.

You can limit or disable cookies in your browser settings. Some website features may work differently as a result.

04

Third-party services and links

The website may link to or use services including Google Maps, Google Analytics, Google AdSense, social media platforms, appointment-form processing, and payment providers.

Those services operate under their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites.

05

Security and storage

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, including encrypted website connections and limited access to personal information. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services and meet legal or operational requirements.

06

Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may ask to access, correct, or delete your personal information; withdraw consent; object to certain processing; or request a portable copy.

Contact us using the details below to make a request. We may need to verify your identity before completing it.

07

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our practices, services, or legal obligations change. The updated date at the top of this page will show when the latest version took effect.
