Good to know

Before you
get inked.

Straight answers to the questions we hear most often.

What is the tattoo process like?+

We begin with a consultation about your idea, placement, and size. After the design is agreed, we prepare the area, transfer the stencil, and tattoo in a sterile environment while checking your comfort throughout.

How do I care for a new tattoo?+

Keep it protected for the period your artist recommends, wash gently with mild soap, pat dry, and use the recommended aftercare. Avoid swimming, soaking, scratching, and direct sunlight while it heals.

Can a tattoo cover a scar?+

Often, yes—but it depends on the scar's age, type, and condition. It should be fully healed. An artist will assess it in person and recommend the safest approach.

How much will my tattoo cost?+

Pricing depends on size, detail, placement, colour, and session time. The guide above is a starting point; we provide a clear quote after understanding your design.

Do tattoos hurt?+

Sensitivity varies by person and placement. Most clients describe it as uncomfortable but manageable, and our artists work carefully to keep the experience as comfortable as possible.
